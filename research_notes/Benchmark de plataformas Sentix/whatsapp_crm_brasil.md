# WhatsApp multi-agent / conversational CRM platforms in Brazil (benchmark for Sentix)

All URLs were fetched or surfaced via search on **2026-09-22** unless a different "seen"/article date is noted. Prices are as published on that date; "older data" is flagged. Currency: BRL unless stated. Where vendors publish in USD, the USD figure is given and no conversion is invented.

---

## KQ1 — Which players are relevant in 2025–2026, and what is each one's positioning?

### Takeaway
The Brazilian market splits into four tiers: (a) global/regional CRM-first tools with WhatsApp inboxes (Kommo, Bitrix24, SleekFlow); (b) Brazilian "multiatendimento" platforms built around the official API (Digisac, Umbler Talk, Poli Digital, Huggy, Multi360, ChatGuru); (c) enterprise CX/BSP platforms (Blip, Zenvia, Octadesk, RD Station Conversas); and (d) a long tail of cheap QR-code (Baileys/Evolution API) SaaS and white-labels (BotConversa, Whaticket, Chatwoot BR, "Zap*" CRMs, Meets). Only tier (d) and a few hybrids (ChatGuru "WEB", Meets, Whaticket, BotConversa Starter) still connect a salesperson's own number by QR code; the rest are official-API-only.

### Inferences
- The market is converging on official-API-only for anything above ~R$500/mo; QR-code connection survives mainly in the sub-R$300 tier and in white-label kits, which is exactly where Sentix's "personal-number" wedge (if any) will be contested on price rather than features.
- Kommo, Umbler Talk, Meets and Whaticket are the closest feature matches to the Sentix brief (kanban + distribution + WhatsApp inbox + AI); Blip/Zenvia/Octadesk/RD are enterprise CX and not price-comparable.
- RD Station Conversas' "unlimited users, pay per client served" and Octadesk's DAU model are notable alternatives to per-seat pricing.

### Gaps
- Digisac's current stance on QR-code (unofficial) connections could not be verified (manual page moved; only "official Cloud API" is documented in 2026 sources).
- No source found for "Zap Comercial"; the name may be a generic term rather than a product.
- Ownership of RD Station (widely reported as a TOTVS company) and Octadesk's ownership were not verified in this research — treat as unconfirmed.
- Huggy's official pricing page returned HTTP 403; figures rely on a third-party review.

## KQ3 — Connection method: QR code (unofficial) vs official Cloud API; can the seller's personal number be used?

### Takeaway
Official-API-only: Digisac, Umbler Talk, Poli, Huggy, Blip, Zenvia, Octadesk, RD Station, SleekFlow, NI, Cloudia. Both: ChatGuru ("API Oficial ou WEB"), Meets (QR lines + API), Whaticket (Baileys QR + API), BotConversa (QR on Starter/Pro, API on Pro+API), Chatwoot BR, Evolution-API SaaS. Kommo discontinued its native QR "WhatsApp Lite"; personal numbers now only via external providers or Meta "coexistence".

### Cited Findings
- Kommo: "A conexão de número pessoal por QR (o WhatsApp Lite, que era um recurso do Kommo), usada durante anos, foi encerrada"; two routes remain — official Cloud API (allows outbound to base) or a "gray" personal number via external QR provider; Cloud API "com telefone" (coexistence) keeps the device usable — [Ventas Boost, 17 Jun 2026](https://ventas-boost.com/pt/blog/conectar-whatsapp-no-kommo/)
- Kommo advises the number must not be logged into WhatsApp on the phone during integration or conflicts can lead to blocking (from a Reclame Aqui complaint) — [Reclame Aqui: número banido após integração com Kommo](https://www.reclameaqui.com.br/kommo_1411514/numero-banido-pelo-whatsapp-apos-integracao-com-kommo-prejuizo-profissional-e-falha-grave-das-plataformas_eMrgiSiN4o8XS2tz/)
- Kommo plan limits: 1 / 3 / unlimited WhatsApp numbers on Básico / Avançado / Pro — [Kommo compare-planos](https://www.kommo.com/br/precos/compare-planos/)
- ChatGuru: WhatsApp Web (multi-agent on unofficial number) and official Meta API, plus proprietary CoEX — [AI Hub Brasil](https://botaihub.com.br/ferramentas/chatguru/)
- Umbler Talk: "uses the official WhatsApp API exclusively (not QR code)" — [Umbler Talk site](https://a.umbler.com/br/talk/)

## KQ7 — Meta Cloud API pricing in Brazil (2025–2026) and policy risk for unofficial QR tools

### Takeaway
Since 1 Jul 2025 Meta bills per delivered template: Brazil marketing ≈US$0.0625, utility and authentication ≈US$0.0068 (sources conflict; one BSP page shows US$0.07188 / US$0.00782 "valid until 30 Sep 2026"); service replies are free inside the 24h window until **1 Oct 2026**, when free-form and in-window utility messages become billable at the utility rate; BRL invoicing for new WABAs began 1 Jul 2026 (migration deadline 30 Jun 2027). Unofficial (Baileys/QR) automation violates WhatsApp ToS with a "when, not if" ban profile, and from 15 Jan 2026 Meta also bars general-purpose AI assistants on the API (business bots remain allowed).

### Cited Findings
- Per-message model: "You are only charged when a template message is delivered", by category and recipient country; conversation-based pricing ended 1 Jul 2025; utility free inside the 24h customer-service window; service (non-template) free; free-entry-point (Click-to-WhatsApp) window 72h; volume tiers for utility/authentication reset monthly — [Meta developers: Pricing](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/pricing)
- BRL billing: "Effective July 1, 2026" eligible Brazilian businesses can create WABAs in BRL, invoiced by Facebook Brasil; migration deadline 30 Jun 2027; all eligible providers by 16 Jul 2026 — [Meta developers: Pricing](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/pricing)
- Brazil rates: marketing US$0.0625; utility US$0.0068; authentication US$0.0068 — [FormBeep rate table](https://formbeep.com/whatsapp-api-pricing/); Courier states these rates "became effective July 1, 2026" and that on **1 Oct 2026** free-form replies in the 24h window (free since Nov 2024) and in-window utility templates (free since Jul 2025) become billable at the utility/auth rate (US$0.0068 in Brazil; US$0.0034 US/CA; US$0.0014 India), an estimated 14% bill increase — [Courier blog](https://www.courier.com/blog/whatsapp-pricing-changes-october-2026); Meta's own notice page for these updates (launching 1 Aug and 1 Oct 2026) — [Meta: Upcoming pricing updates for Meta Business Agent, service and utility messages](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/pricing/non-template-messages)
- Conflicting BSP figure: SleekFlow lists Brazil marketing US$0.07188, utility US$0.00782, authentication US$0.00782, "valid until September 30, 2026" — [SleekFlow help](https://help.sleekflow.io/pt_BR/whatsapp/pricing) (may include markup or reflect an earlier rate card)
- BRL approximations (secondary, 21 May 2026): utility R$0.04–0.05, authentication R$0.15–0.19, marketing R$0.31–0.38; BSP markups 10–30%; >500k msgs/mo BSPs discount 15–30% for annual commits — [Message Central](https://www.messagecentral.com/blog/whatsapp-business-api-pricing-brazil) (its authentication figure looks outdated vs the US$0.0068 rate)
- Older-era reference: Cloudia FAQ still cites US$1 per 1,000 bot messages and US$0.0483 per proactive message (conversation-era pricing; flag as old) — [Cloudia FAQ](https://cloudia.com.br/faq/)
- Vendor pass-through examples: BotConversa resells templates at R$0,50 marketing / R$0,05 utility — [Tudo pra Whats](https://tudoprawhats.com.br/botconversa); Zenvia WhatsApp template US$0.0396–0.0463 — [Zenvia prices](https://zenvia.com/en/prices/); RD wallet min R$300 at Meta rates — [RD planos](https://www.rdstation.com/planos/conversas/)
- Unofficial tools: "Using unauthorized third-party software to send automated or bulk messages is a direct violation of WhatsApp's Terms of Service… 131 Chrome extensions were caught mass-sending on WhatsApp in 2025"; permanent bans rarely appealed — [bot.space](https://www.bot.space/blog/whatsapp-api-vs-unofficial-tools-a-complete-risk-reward-analysis-for-2025); [ask4lead](https://ask4lead.com/will-whatsapp-ban-you-for-automating-your-business/)
- Baileys-mode ban mechanics: bans triggered by user reports ("2–3 complaints within short periods"), gray numbers "safe for inbound-only workflows", high risk for cold outreach — [Ventas Boost, 17 Jun 2026](https://ventas-boost.com/pt/blog/conectar-whatsapp-no-kommo/); [Cubo Suite](https://blog.cubosuite.com.br/evolution-api-guia-completo/)
- AI policy (effective 15 Jan 2026): Meta bars third-party general-purpose AI chatbots (ChatGPT, Copilot, Perplexity) from the Business API; "automation tied to business workflows (customer service, orders, tracking, FAQs) remains permitted under official WhatsApp Business API"; reverse-engineered integrations (Baileys) also listed as non-compliant — [Chatboq, 28 Apr 2026](https://chatboq.com/blogs/third-party-ai-chatbots-ban); [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/technology/no-more-chatgpt-and-perplexity-on-whatsapp-meta-bans-major-ai-chatbots-from-2026-1.500316016)

### Inferences
- For Sentix cost modelling, assume Brazil ≈US$0.0625 marketing and ≈US$0.0068 utility/auth per delivered template, plus from 1 Oct 2026 ≈US$0.0068 per free-form reply inside the 24h window — the "service is free" pitch used by many vendors (Octadesk, RD, Digisac copy) will become stale.
- Because the 2026 AI policy explicitly whitelists business-workflow bots, AI reply suggestions/analysis inside a CRM are compliant; a general "chat with an AI" feature exposed to end customers on WhatsApp is not.

### Gaps
- Meta's official Brazil rate card (CSV/XLSX) was not fetched directly; the US$0.0625 / US$0.0068 figures come from aggregators and one BSP, with a conflicting US$0.07188 / US$0.00782 set. Verify against Meta's rate card before publishing.
- Exact BRL rates under the new BRL invoicing (post 1 Jul 2026) were not found.
- Meta's "coexistence" (Cloud API + phone app) documentation and eligibility in Brazil not verified from a primary source.
