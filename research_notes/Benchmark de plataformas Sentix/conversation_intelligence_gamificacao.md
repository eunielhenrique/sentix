# Conversation Intelligence & Sales Gamification — benchmark fact sheet for Sentix

Date seen for all sources: 2026-09-22 (unless the source itself carries another date, noted inline). Currency as published; BRL figures are from Brazilian vendor/aggregator pages. Items marked (secondary) come from third-party pricing guides/aggregators, not the vendor's own page — treat as indicative.

## KQ1. Global conversation-intelligence players: pricing, contract terms, phone-call vs. WhatsApp coverage

### Takeaway
Global CI is priced on two tiers: enterprise suites (Gong, Chorus, Salesloft, Modjo, Jiminny) at roughly USD 85–135/user/month with platform fees, minimum seats and annual/multi-year contracts; and self-serve meeting-recorder tools (Fireflies, Avoma, tl;dv) at USD 10–50/user/month. All analyze meetings/phone calls via dialer or video integrations; none of the global players natively analyzes WhatsApp chats — that gap is where Brazilian tools (Koee, RD Conversas, Agendor Ava) and Sentix sit.

### Cited Findings
**Gong**
- List price ~USD 1,600/user/year; volume discounts to USD 1,200–1,400; mandatory platform fee USD 5,000/yr (small teams) to USD 50,000+ (enterprise); onboarding USD 7,500–28,500+; all plans annual, most customers on multi-year; modules = Conversation Intelligence (core), Engage (reportedly doubles per-user cost), Forecast; 2026 adds "Gong Credits", a usage meter for AI processing (transcription, coaching, AI agents) on top of seats. Article dated July 6, 2026 (secondary) — [tl;dv Gong pricing guide](https://tldv.io/blog/gong-pricing/)
- Foundation (Core only) USD 1,298–1,426/user/yr (USD 108–119/mo); bundled Core+Engage+Forecast USD 2,880–3,000/user/yr; no free tier, no monthly billing, auto-renewal uplift (secondary) — [Vendr Gong marketplace](https://www.vendr.com/marketplace/gong); [MarketBetter breakdown](https://marketbetter.ai/blog/gong-pricing-breakdown-2026/)
- Language: Gong supports 70+ languages; "Portuguese (Brazil, Portugal)" is listed for calls and transcripts. Some AI features "only generate results for calls in English" (email labels, red-flag alerts, Gong-recommended to-dos, example-based smart trackers); question-based smart trackers work in all languages — [Gong Help Center: language support](https://help.gong.io/docs/what-languages-are-supported-in-gong)

### Inferences
- The floor for "serious" CI is ~USD 100/user/month plus fees; the ceiling for self-serve recorders is ~USD 50. A Brazilian product priced in BRL between these bands (say R$ 150–400/user/month) undercuts every global suite by 3–5x while matching Avoma-with-CI-add-on (USD 48–74 total) in USD terms.
- Gong's own "Gong Credits" (2026) shows even the market leader is moving AI processing to usage-based billing — relevant for how Sentix prices transcription minutes.
- Gong's Portuguese support is transcription-complete but AI-feature-partial (several coaching features English-only) — a concrete pt-BR differentiation claim, if Sentix's rubric scoring is fully native in Portuguese.

### Gaps

- Could not confirm whether any global player (Gong/Salesloft) ingests WhatsApp Business API messages; searches surfaced none. Treat "no WhatsApp" as unconfirmed absence, not verified.

## KQ2. Brazil/LatAm players: what they analyze (phone vs WhatsApp), AI approach, pricing

In Brazil, call transcription + AI scoring already exists inside sales-engagement/CRM suites (PipeRun scores calls by SPIN/BANT with per-seller tips; Exact Spotter transcribes and summarizes with next steps; Meetime IA summarizes calls via OpenAI), while WhatsApp conversation scoring exists as a niche add-on (Koee: quality/sentiment/SLA score per conversation, R$ 197/mo for 5 agents). ## KQ3. What the platforms score and whether they deliver per-seller coaching plans

### Takeaway

### Cited Findings

- Gong benchmark: high-performing B2B sellers have a talk-to-listen ratio around 60:40; lost enterprise deals mention "security review" twice as often as won deals (secondary summaries of Gong Labs) — [Revenue.io AI coaching platforms](https://www.revenue.io/blog/best-ai-sales-coaching-platforms); [Allego CI guide](https://www.allego.com/blog/conversation-intelligence/)

- Fireflies: talk-time, sentiment, question detection, topic trackers — [Fireflies pricing](https://fireflies.ai/pricing)
- PipeRun: score per SPIN/BANT criterion, strengths/weaknesses, practical tips per call to the seller — [PipeRun IA ligações](https://crmpiperun.com/crm-de-vendas/ia-ligacoes-vendas/)
- Exact Spotter: summary, highlights, insights, next steps, feedback field — [Exact help](https://exactsales.freshdesk.com/pt-BR/support/solutions/articles/73000622280-an%C3%A1lise-de-ligac%C3%B5es-transcric%C3%A3o-de-ligac%C3%b5es)

Batch STT for Portuguese now costs USD 0.0025–0.006/min at the major APIs (AssemblyAI Universal-2 USD 0.15/hr, Deepgram Nova-3 multilingual USD 0.0052/min, OpenAI whisper-1/gpt-4o-transcribe USD 0.006/min, gpt-4o-mini-transcribe USD 0.003/min; Google STT V2 is the outlier at USD 0.016/min), and Gemini can transcribe natively from audio tokens at ~USD 0.0015/min (2.5 Flash) or ~USD 0.00045/min (Flash-Lite). ### Inferences (worked cost model, 10-minute pt-BR call; my arithmetic from the prices above)
- STT for 10 min: AssemblyAI Universal-2 USD 0.025 (+USD 0.003 diarization); Universal-3.5 Pro USD 0.035; Deepgram Nova-3 multilingual USD 0.052 (Growth USD 0.043); gpt-4o-mini-transcribe USD 0.030; whisper-1 / gpt-4o-transcribe USD 0.060; Google V2 USD 0.160; Gemini 2.5 Flash direct audio ≈ 15,000 audio tokens × USD 1.00/M = USD 0.015 + ~2k output tokens USD 0.005 ≈ USD 0.020; Gemini 2.5 Flash-Lite ≈ USD 0.0045 + 0.0008 ≈ USD 0.005.
- Rubric scoring of the transcript (≈5k input tokens incl. rubric prompt, ≈1k output): Gemini 2.5 Flash-Lite ≈ USD 0.001; Gemini 2.5 Flash ≈ USD 0.004; Claude Haiku 4.5 ≈ USD 0.010; GPT-5.1 ≈ USD 0.016; Claude Sonnet 5 ≈ USD 0.020.
- All-in per 10-min call: cheapest stack (Gemini Flash-Lite audio + Flash-Lite scoring) ≈ USD 0.006; balanced stack (AssemblyAI Universal-2 + Gemini 2.5 Flash) ≈ USD 0.03; premium (Deepgram Nova-3 + Claude Sonnet 5) ≈ USD 0.07–0.09. At an illustrative R$ 5/USD that is R$ 0.03–0.45 per call — i.e. a seller doing 30 calls/day (~600/month) costs R$ 20–270/month in AI, well under any plausible seat price.
- WhatsApp chats need no STT: a 40-message chat (~2–3k tokens) scored on Gemini 2.5 Flash costs < USD 0.005; on Claude Haiku 4.5 ~USD 0.01.
- Gemini's native audio path (no separate STT vendor) is the cheapest and simplest for pt-BR, but there is no independent pt-BR WER benchmark to confirm quality parity with Deepgram/AssemblyAI — pilot on real call audio before committing.

### Gaps
- OpenAI's official pricing page could not be fetched (403); OpenAI figures are from aggregators dated Sep 2026.
- No Brazilian-Portuguese WER benchmark found for any engine (all published comparisons are English or code-switching).
- AssemblyAI's pricing page did not list language coverage; Portuguese support must be verified on its docs.
- Google STT pricing page content was truncated; USD 0.016/min comes from Google's blog + aggregators.

Brazilian options (Gamefic ~R$ 50/user, Funifier from USD 1/user reported) are cheaper but generic — none is built around conversation scores.

## KQ6. Evidence on effectiveness: gamification on activity, AI coaching on conversion

### Takeaway
Rigorous evidence is thin: one peer-reviewed field experiment (n=147 reps) shows gamified virtual sales training raises enjoyment, motivation and declarative sales knowledge; the headline sales-outcome numbers (+25% conversion, +127% appointments, +50/35/26% win rates) are vendor-reported (Spinify, Hoopla/Comcast, Gong Labs) and should be labelled as such.

### Cited Findings
- Peer-reviewed: field experiment with 147 sales reps found gamification in virtual sales training improves enjoyment, which increases training motivation and acquisition of declarative sales knowledge (Journal of Personal Selling & Sales Management, 2024/2025; full text 403) — [Tandfonline article](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/08853134.2024.2431817); [ResearchGate record](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/388929580_Gamification_in_virtual_sales_training_evidence_from_a_field_experiment)
- Gong Labs (vendor, published Feb 15 2024, updated Mar 6 2026; >1M opportunities, 1,418 orgs): win rates +50% when reps completed all AI next-best-actions; +26% when "Ask Anything" used in a deal; +35% with Smart Trackers; reps spend 12.1 h/week on email, AI frees ~6 h — [Gong Labs ROI of AI](https://www.gong.io/blog/we-measured-the-roi-of-ai-in-sales-heres-how-it-really-impacts-your-deals)
- Attributed to "Gartner 2026 Sales Enablement Report": real-time AI coaching improves win rates 8–12% within three months; new hires productive 30–50% faster (secondary; original not retrieved) — [Revenue.io AI coaching platforms](https://www.revenue.io/blog/best-ai-sales-coaching-platforms)
- Vendor/aggregator gamification claims: contests/leaderboards linked to 25%+ increase in conversion; "McKinsey ~20% sales performance improvement"; Comcast contest via integrated tool +127% daily appointments vs manual contest; 13-point forecast-accuracy gain and 23% faster time-to-first-deal after moving to behaviour-based gamification; US teams 40% productivity uplift — [Hoopla sales gamification](https://hoopla.net/sales-gamification-how-leading-companies-turn-sales-performance-into-a-daily-game/); [Late for Break Substack](https://lateforbreak.substack.com/p/benefits-of-gamification-for-remote); [AmplifAI gamification statistics](https://www.amplifai.com/blog/gamification-statistics)
- Spinify's stats roundup (2025) cites: Gartner "over 70% of Global 2000 companies have adopted gamification"; TalentLMS 89% prefer gamified learning; Zippia 90% feel more motivated, +48% engagement; Aberdeen Group +4.1% annual net revenue with game mechanics; ServiceNow +60% engagement — years not given — [Spinify statistics](https://spinify.com/blog/sales-gamification-statistics/)
- Cautionary evidence: natural experiment on GitHub showed removing gamification (streaks) changed developer behaviour, indicating gamification effects are real but can drive superficial activity — [arXiv 2006.02371](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2006.02371)

### Inferences
- For Sentix's pitch, the safest quantitative claims are Gong's (large-sample but vendor and correlational) and the n=147 field experiment (causal but about training knowledge, not revenue). Avoid citing the "25% conversion" / "McKinsey 20%" figures without the original reports.

### Gaps
- Could not retrieve the Gartner 2026 Sales Enablement report or the McKinsey source; the Tandfonline full text was blocked (403), so effect sizes are unavailable.
- No study found measuring AI conversation scoring specifically on WhatsApp sales in Brazil.
